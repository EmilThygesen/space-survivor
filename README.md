# Space Survivor

A small, offline game built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No dependencies or build step.

## Play

Open `index.html` in Edge, Chrome, or Firefox, choose **Level 1** on the main screen, then select **Deploy Level 1**. Level 1 is selected by default; Levels 2 and 3 are grayed out, disabled, and marked **comming soon**.

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. On a touchscreen, drag the joystick.
- **Level 1 lasts 9 waves**: eight increasingly difficult swarm waves, followed by the final **Overlord** on wave 9. Defeat it to win; there is no wave 10. The main-screen selector previews Levels 2 and 3, but they are not playable yet.
- Swarm waves spawn **three times the previously doubled enemy counts** (six times the original counts): **72 on wave 1**, increasing to **240 on wave 8**. Enemies spawn **50% faster than the previous 2.5x rate**, or **3.75x the original rate**: intervals are divided by 3.75, approximately **0.171 seconds on wave 1** and **0.118 seconds on wave 8**. The 85-live-enemy cap still queues excess spawns. Enemy-type probabilities and the 15-second boss schedule are unchanged. Wave 9 still contains just the final Overlord.
- During each wave, the wave badge shows **ENEMIES LEFT**, counting both enemies waiting to spawn and living enemies, including additional timed bosses. Spawning does not reduce the total; kills do. The final wave counts the single Overlord. The counter freezes while paused and gives way to the countdown between waves. Enemy fireballs are not enemies, so a wave with zero enemies can still require dodging the remaining projectiles before it clears.
- Your soldier has **exactly two arms, each holding one gun**. Both guns **focus fire on a living boss within the shared weapon range**, even if normal enemies are closer. This includes the final Overlord; if multiple bosses are in range, both guns choose the boss nearest the soldier. Without an in-range boss, the guns automatically aim at **two different living enemies whenever at least two are in range**, including chickens and skeletons. They prefer nearby targets for each gun; if both prefer the same enemy, one switches to another nearby enemy. They share a non-boss target only when it is the sole living target in range. Normal two-target aiming resumes when the boss dies or leaves range. Shots use compact, pointed bullet sprites without tracer trails and small muzzle flashes. The outer gold circle shows the shared range; bullets stop at its limit.
- Weapon range starts at **360 map units**. The armory's **Weapon range** upgrade adds **40 units per level**, up to **960 units at level 15**. Its first level costs 25 gold; later levels cost more.
- **Fire rate** now supports **25 upgrade levels**, ten more than the previous cap of 15. Existing levels are preserved. Each level still increases firing rate by **12%**, with the same **30-gold** first purchase and 38% price growth per level (rounded up).
- **Projectile speed** is a permanent armory upgrade starting at **50 gold**, with later prices increasing by 38% per level (rounded up). Soldier bullets start at **1,080 map units per second**; each level adds **20**, up to **1,380 at level 15**. It affects every bullet from both guns, including dual and critical bullets, without changing damage, weapon range, fire rate, or enemy fireball speed. The current projectile speed appears under Fire rate in the HUD. Purchases apply from your next run.
- **Crit Chance %** is a permanent armory upgrade starting at **500 gold**, with later prices increasing by 38% per level (rounded up). Each level adds **5 percentage points** of critical chance, from 0% up to **50% at level 10**. Each bullet rolls independently to deal **double its upgraded damage**, including dual shots from both guns. Critical bullets and `CRIT` damage numbers are pink. The current chance appears under Bullet damage in the HUD. Purchases apply from your next run; chicken explosions and skeleton fireballs cannot critically hit.
- **Health regeneration** is a permanent armory upgrade starting at **100 gold**, with later prices increasing by 38% per level (rounded up). There is no regeneration until you buy the first level, which unlocks **0.5 HP per second**. Each upgrade adds another **0.5 HP per second**, capped at **5 HP per second at level 10**. It heals only during active play, including between-wave countdowns, never exceeds your upgraded maximum health, and stops while paused or after a run ends. Purchases apply from your next run. The current rate appears beneath Suit integrity in the HUD; healing does not subtract from damage-taken statistics.
- Each gun initially fires **one bullet**, for **two bullets per volley**. The armory's **Dual bullets** upgrade is a **one-time, 2,500-gold** purchase for **two simultaneous bullets per gun**, capped at **four total per volley**. There is no third-bullet tier. Upgrades never add arms or guns. Each gun's parallel bullets deal your full upgraded damage and keep your normal weapon range and shared fire-rate cooldown. The upgrade is permanent and applies starting with your next run.
- **Wave gold collection**, beside Dual bullets in the armory, is a **one-time, 2,500-gold** upgrade. Starting with your next run, clearing each wave banks **all remaining gold coins and red boss crystals anywhere on the map**, including the final enemy's drop, then animates them flying along glowing, curved trails to your soldier. Crystals stay red during collection. Rewards are banked immediately, so pausing or ending the run mid-animation cannot lose gold. Normal pickup still works; unused medkits disappear rather than being collected automatically. It activates only after all enemies and incoming fireballs are gone, not when you die or retreat.
- After purchasing Wave gold collection, use its **ON/OFF button in the armory** or **Wave gold: ON/OFF in the game footer** to toggle it for future wave clears, including during a run. The setting is saved, and turning it off does not remove the upgrade or cost gold to re-enable. Already-started collection animations finish normally. Coin flights now run at **half their previous speed**, taking twice as long, without delaying gold rewards. Pausing freezes coin flights; reduced-motion mode uses short, straight flights without trails.
- Wave 1 keeps its original normal-alien stats: **26 HP, 61 speed, and 8 attack damage**. Later waves scale base health by **30% per wave**, add **6 speed per wave**, and scale attack damage by **20% per wave** (health and damage are rounded). Enemy counts and spawn pressure also increase.
- A large orange boss spawns every **15 seconds of active play** during the swarm waves, with **20x the current wave's normal alien health** and **4x its movement speed**. Each boss drops **one red crystal worth 150 gold**, instead of a gold coin, regardless of wave or boss strength. Pausing stops the timer; each new run resets it. Timed bosses stop once wave 8 is cleared, so the finale is a single-boss encounter.
- The giant **Overlord** has **30x the health of a normal wave-9 boss** (600x a normal wave-9 alien), a 76-unit body radius, and **210 movement speed**, below your soldier's unupgraded 245 speed. It chases you and, within **900 units**, marks a fixed landing zone at your current position. React quickly: the **0.5-second warning** and **0.25-second leap** leave only **0.75 seconds** to escape the **115-unit landing blast**. It does not home in after marking the target, cannot hit you while airborne, and pauses for **0.65 seconds** after landing before recovering; its next jump has a **3-second cooldown**. The slam deals wave-9 attack damage. Pausing freezes every attack phase. Its **150-gold red crystal** is banked immediately on defeat, even without automatic collection, so the victory screen cannot strand the reward.
- Winning opens a **Level 1 complete** screen with your whole run's **aliens killed**, **bullets fired**, and **damage taken**, plus completion time and collected gold. Bullets count individual projectiles from both guns: two or four per volley. Damage counts only HP actually lost, excluding invulnerability-blocked hits and excess lethal damage; healing never subtracts from the total. Replay starts Level 1 from wave 1 with your permanent upgrades, or return to the armory.
- Explosive chickens make up roughly 20% of normal wave spawns. They stop and light a **1.5-second fuse** when they get within 90 map units of you, and also **explode immediately when killed**, even before their fuse is lit. Blasts damage both you and nearby enemies within the 120-unit radius, dealing three times the wave's normal contact damage. Killing another chicken with a blast starts a chain reaction. Shoot chickens from a safe distance to use them against the swarm. Chickens do not deal contact damage; their fuse stays lit if you move away but freezes while paused. Every enemy killed by a blast awards normal kill credit and loot.
- Bone-white **skeletons appear from wave 3**, making up roughly **15%** of normal spawns without replacing chickens or bosses. They approach or retreat to stay about 280 units away and attack within 520 units. A glowing staff, `CASTING` label, and charge bar warn you **0.45 seconds before firing**, now **every 1 second**, twice the previous firing rate. Fireballs aim at your position when fired and travel straight at 260 units/second, so you can dodge them; they expire after 4 seconds or leaving the arena. Skeletons have **1.3x normal health**, **0.85x movement speed**, and fireballs deal **1.5x normal attack damage**, all using the current wave's stats. They deal no contact damage. Shoot them or use chicken blasts to kill them for strength-based gold; already-fired fireballs stay dangerous until they hit or expire. Waves clear once enemies and incoming fireballs are gone. Pausing freezes casting and projectiles; ending or restarting a run clears them.
- Walk near gold coins or red boss crystals to collect them. Both use the same pickup-radius upgrade; the smaller teal circle around your soldier shows that radius. Crystals convert directly into saved gold rather than introducing a separate currency.
- **Non-boss** gold rewards scale with each enemy's maximum health and its speed/attack bonuses, rather than a small random reward, and retain **double their original value**, including skeletons. A wave-1 normal alien drops **4 gold** and a chicken **8**; stronger non-boss enemies in later waves pay more. Their existing rounded reward is doubled, unless limited by the currency cap. **Bosses instead award one red crystal worth a fixed 150 gold.** Coins can be worth multiple gold, shown as `+N` when collected; manual crystal pickups show their gold value in red. Wounded enemies and kills caused by chicken explosions still pay their full reward.
- Clearing a swarm wave restores 12% of your maximum health. Green medkits restore 22%; unused medkits disappear as soon as a wave clears, including the finale. Medkits dropped during an intermission also disappear when the next wave starts, while uncollected coins and crystals retain their normal pickup or wave-collection behavior.
- Press **P** or **Esc** to pause. You can end a run early from the pause menu.

After a run, spend gold in the armory to improve **bullet damage**, **fire rate**, **projectile speed**, **weapon range**, **run speed**, **maximum health**, **health regeneration**, **gold pickup radius**, and **critical chance**, or unlock **dual bullets** and **automatic wave gold collection**. Compact armory cards show **four upgrades per row on desktop** and **two on smaller screens**. Upgrades apply to the next run and are permanent. The armory's **Deploy again** button returns to the main screen for level selection; the next run starts only when you select **Deploy Level 1** there.

Collected gold, upgrades, and records save automatically in this browser's local storage. Use the same browser and file location to continue your progress. Private browsing or clearing browser data can remove the save. If saving is blocked, the game displays a warning rather than pretending progress was saved.

Existing saves retain their gold, other upgrades, and records, including best-wave records from longer versions of Level 1. Missing projectile-speed, weapon-range, critical-chance, health-regeneration, dual-bullet, and wave-gold-collection upgrades start at level zero. Previously purchased dual bullets stay unlocked with two bullets **per gun**. Saves with the former third-bullet tier migrate to this new four-bullet total cap without resetting their remaining progress. Wave gold collection stays unlocked if already purchased, without an additional charge, and its new toggle defaults to ON.

Sound and fullscreen can be toggled in the interface. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Development

`game-core.js` contains the simulation and upgrade rules. `game.js` handles rendering, controls, menus, audio, and browser saves.

With Node.js installed, run `npm test` to exercise the simulation and upgrade rules. No installation is needed.
